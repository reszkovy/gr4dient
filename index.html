About

gr4dient is a free gradient generator that runs in your browser. Build mesh gradients from up to five colours, switch to flowing, folded or light-beam modes, add grain or dither, and export a PNG or a looping MP4 for backgrounds, slides, social posts and websites.

Motion is tied to tempo: set the BPM and the number of bars, and the loop ends exactly on the bar, so it cuts cleanly to music. No sign-up, nothing is uploaded, the link keeps your settings.

Open source under the MIT license. Code, architecture notes and tests: github.com/reszkovy/gr4dient
